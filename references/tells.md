# The tells: what makes it look AI-made

Ranked from most to least damaging. Every item is forbidden. Review each row against the 4 fps contact sheets and the audio checks before sign-off.

| Rank | Forbidden | Check |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Invented evidence**: a generated or retyped headline, document, filing, chart value, map, quote or "archival" photo | Every artifact traces to a README link with identical wording and values (`sourcing-licences.md` §8.2, §8.3) |
| 2 | **Real people's likeness generated**, or identifiable generated faces presented as real | No generated asset depicts a named person; no identifiable generated face (§8.4) |
| 3 | **Wrong frame cadence**: everything smooth on ones (the "floaty AI" look), or real footage posterized | `motion.md` §4.1 cadence classifier: graphics ≥50% on twos/threes, footage ≥90% on ones |
| 4 | **Pop entrances**: scale from 0, bounce, elastic, spin, overshoot; or everything arriving at once | `motion.md` §4.2 and §4.3 frame steps |
| 5 | **Flat collage**: one generated scene cut apart, a single plane with a Ken Burns zoom where depth was needed, exposed plate edges | `look.md` §3.6 displacement ratio 1.5-3; parallax shot count; no exposed edges |
| 6 | **Cut-out defects**: halo or chroma fringe, floating limbs, broken hands, inconsistent shadow angles, objects with no contact | `look.md` §3.5 200% edge review and spill sample |
| 7 | **AI-default typography**: banned fonts, >2 families, outlined or shadowed captions, giant bouncing captions, emoji | `look.md` §3.2 font list, `captions-title.md` §6.1 |
| 8 | **AI-default colour**: saturated multicolour, blue-purple neon, gradients, glow, pure `#FFFFFF` fields, accent overuse | `look.md` §3.1 accent share 2-6% mean, saturation ≤0.30, glow/gradient review |
| 9 | **Topic clichés**: glowing brains, robots, circuit-board overlays, holographic UIs, blue-lit server aisles as mood, floating icons standing in for the subject, warning symbols as decoration, stock disaster imagery | Frame review on 4 fps sheets: zero instances |
| 10 | **Global filters**: VHS, heavy film grain, full-frame halftone, film burn, light leaks, fake dust and scratches on vector graphics | `look.md` §3.3 patch std 3-8 levels; review for global effects |
| 11 | **Highlighter wrong**: Normal blend greying the text, out of sync, several phrases at once | `evidence-graphics.md` §5.2 ±3 f and ink luminance ≤60 |
| 12 | **Perfect geometry where hand marks belong**: closed perfect circles, glowing or thick annotation strokes | `evidence-graphics.md` §5.3 |
| 13 | **Decorative maps and charts**: no labels, no units, no source, default map tiles, raster pixelation on zoom, charts in cards, 3D bars | `evidence-graphics.md` §5.6, §5.7 |
| 14 | **Static slides**: frozen frames >2 s, uniform Ken Burns on every shot | `motion.md` §4.4 static detector |
| 15 | **TTS cadence**: uniform pauses, flat delivery, mispronounced names or acronyms, WPM outside 160-190 | `sound.md` §7.1 gap std ≥0.12 s; transcript match |
| 16 | **Audio over-design**: music fighting the VO, whoosh or riser on every cut, braams, typewriter clatter, clipping, silent holes | `sound.md` §7.2-§7.4 |
| 17 | **Weak open**: logo sting, silent intro, a question or "In this video" opener, first word after 0.3 s | `story-script.md` §2.2 |
| 18 | **Weak end**: outro montage, call to action, logo card, music truncated, fade before the last word | `story-script.md` §2.5 |
| 19 | **Repetition**: the same composition >2 times, one visual mode >12 s | `motion.md` §4.6 |
| 20 | **Off-era or off-style devices**: hatched or scribbled shadows, riso misregistration, ink-bleed or page-curl transitions, rack-focus on every cut, sticker borders >12 px, rounded "app" chips | Frame review against `look.md` §3.5, `motion.md` §4.5, `evidence-graphics.md` §5.5 |
| 21 | **Text errors**: typos, mismatched numbers between VO, captions and graphics, text in unsafe zones or cut off | `story-script.md` §2.4, `captions-title.md` §6.1, §6.2 |
