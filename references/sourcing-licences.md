# Sourcing and licences

8.1 **Real photos and footage** only from sources whose licence allows this use: public-domain government images (for example national agencies, regulators, statistics offices and space agencies whose works are public domain), Wikimedia Commons files with their stated licence (CC0, CC BY, CC BY-SA, attribution as the licence requires), and official company press-kit images used under their press terms. Each one is logged in the README with URL, author, licence and date accessed.
- Check: every photo or clip on screen has a README row with a licence that permits reuse; no row says "unknown" or "Google Images".

8.2 **Documents, headlines and filings** are captured from the real page (live URL or a dated archive copy). They may be cropped, defocused or have irrelevant lines greyed. Their words, numbers, dates and mastheads are never altered, retyped, translated or composited from parts of different pages.
- Check: each document shot's README row links to the exact page; a reviewer opening the link finds the same headline and highlighted phrase.

8.3 **Data** for charts and maps comes from a named dataset or report (government agencies, industry operators, company filings and disclosures, regulator databases, peer-reviewed studies) with its date. Plotted values match the source to its stated precision.
- Check: `research/sources.md` lists every plotted series with its source table and values.

8.4 **Generated images** are allowed only for: anonymous cut-out subjects (people whose faces are not identifiable: from behind, cropped, silhouetted or at distance), generic objects (a hard hat, a cable reel, a coin stack, a cardboard box), and textures (paper, grain, halftone, grid). Generate each on a flat chroma background and key it clean, one subject per image; never generate a whole collage and cut it apart.
- Generated images are never: documents, headlines, screenshots, charts, maps, logos, signage with words, "archival" photos, a specific real place or facility presented as itself, or any real person's likeness.
- Check: the README marks every generated asset as generated and states what it depicts; none of them fall in the forbidden list; no generated face is identifiable at 100%.

8.5 **Logos** of companies or agencies appear only as they appear inside real documents, press materials or footage, unaltered, used to identify the subject.

8.6 **Music and SFX** are licensed or generated with rights to use; each is credited in the README.

8.7 Credits stay off screen apart from source lines (`evidence-graphics.md` §5.8).

8.8 **Style references are not sources.** The classic explainers studied in the style-research step are for measuring the look only. No frame, graphic, music or audio from them appears in the output.
